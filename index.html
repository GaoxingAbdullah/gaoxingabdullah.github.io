Research Interests

Broad themes that drive my current and future work.

Post-Quantum Security

Designing lattice-based authentication protocols that remain secure against quantum adversaries.

Internet of Medical Things

Building efficient and secure communication frameworks for connected healthcare devices.

AI for Healthcare Security

Applying machine learning to detect intrusions, anomalies, and adversarial attacks in healthcare systems and medical networks.

Federated Learning

Privacy-preserving distributed training across edge devices without sharing raw patient data.

Cybersecurity

Securing IoT/IoMT ecosystems and healthcare infrastructure through threat modelling, secure protocol design, and resilient defence strategies.
